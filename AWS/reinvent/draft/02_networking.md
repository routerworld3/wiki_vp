# Networking & Content Delivery

## Your AWS Networking and Content Delivery guide to re:Invent 2026

*by Rick Armstrong on 26 OCT 2026 in Announcements | Permalink | Share*

The AWS Networking and Content Delivery (CDN) team invites you to join us at re:Invent from November 30 through December 4 2026. During that time, Las Vegas, Nevada becomes the ultimate destination for cloud innovation, and there is no better time to look ahead at where cloud networking is going.

Across five action-packed days you can dive deep into the latest AWS Networking and CDN capabilities, learn about the newest innovations, and connect with industry leaders. With thousands of sessions at your fingertips, you will have the opportunity to sharpen your skills, discover new strategies, and stay ahead of the curve as agentic workloads reshape what the network can do.

### Keynotes

Join AWS leadership as they unveil the most significant announcements, new service launches, and product updates that will shape the future of cloud computing. The keynotes are where major service reveals, infrastructure improvements, and connectivity innovations land, and they set the context for everything you will explore in the networking track.

### Session types

Sessions are available in several formats, and most sit under the Networking and CDN (NET) topic in the event catalog. If you plan to attend in person, lightning talks are walk-up only. For every other format you can reserve a seat through the event portal (login required) or join as a walk-up based on availability.

* **Breakout sessions:** Lecture-style 60-minute presentations led by AWS networking experts and customers.
* **Lightning talks:** 20-minute content on a focused topic. Each networking lightning talk features a recent launch.
* **Chalk talks:** 60-minute interactive sessions where AWS experts lead discussions and whiteboard in real time.
* **Code talks:** 60-minute sessions featuring coding demonstrations and technical implementations.
* **Workshops:** Hands-on, 120-minute sessions where you work directly with AWS services in a guided environment.
* **Builders’ sessions:** One-hour small-group sessions where you build something alongside an AWS expert.
